## When to use
When you want reusable Agent Skills for Copilot in Excel/PowerPoint (and the same files in Claude / ChatGPT / Codex / Gemini CLI): draft board decks, native-shape diagrams, strip AI tone from slides, or audit workbook formulas—always draft-then-stop for human approval.

## What it does
Community library of 27 open `SKILL.md` skills (15 PowerPoint + 12 Excel): outline-first decks, executive-summary slides, action-title rewrites, accessibility pass, reconciliation / formula-risk / variance explainers, and more. Each skill prepares a change and waits; nothing is applied until a person says so. Ships zip installs under `dist/personal/`, org-deploy notes, and runtime docs for non-Microsoft agents.
