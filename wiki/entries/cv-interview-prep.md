# cv-interview-prep（按 JD 改简历 / 面试）

**Tags:** careers, resume, interview, chinese, skill
**Source:** [@HMarcoGao](https://x.com/HMarcoGao/status/2115408437731541267) · 2026-10-28
**Engagement:** Full SKILL.md in oparker daily pack

## When to use
When the user pastes a **resume + job ad** (or asks to tailor a CV, write a cover letter, prep interview Qs, or check fit)—especially Chinese-language job hunt flows.

## What it does
Six-step recipe: ask for missing materials → extract must/nice/keywords/deal-breakers → honest match scoring → rewrite bullets as action+task+result (placeholders, never invent numbers) → ~300-char cover letter → 10 interview Qs with frameworks + 3 company questions. Hard red lines: no fabricating experience, no applying on platforms, no guaranteeing offers.
