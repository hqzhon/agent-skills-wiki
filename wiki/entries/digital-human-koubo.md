## When to use
When you want an agent to turn one photo + a script into a lip-synced AI talking-head video (live-commerce ads, product explainers, tutorials)—with quote-before-spend, per-segment QA, and creator-style packaging—instead of hand-editing in HeyGen-class tools.

## What it does
Agent Skill pack: persona from one photo, script split into ≤15s clips via Renoise MCP (Grok Video / Wan / OmniHuman), whisper + frame QA with redo, spectral/loudness matching across segments, Remotion edit with 5 styles and 48 packaging components. Quote credits before batch; preview segment 1 first.
