# geometric-logo-construction（几何 Logo 构造）

**Engagement:** MIT · full construction method + ratio cheat-sheet

## When to use
When someone wants a **geometric / “perfect” constructed logo or symbol**, or a construction sheet showing how the mark was built—works with Figma, any vector editor, or plain SVG.

## What it does
Nine-step method: concept → modular grid → circle/line primitives with deliberate ratios (1:2, √2, φ, Fibonacci…) → boolean shapes → optical correction (logged) → construction sheet → applications → self-check → recommend. Ratio cheat-sheet and construction-sheet spec included.
