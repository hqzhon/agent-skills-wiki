## When to use
When the user pastes **income/bills** and asks where money went / can I afford X / debt repayment order—Chinese personal-finance snapshot only.

## What it does
Classify income/expenses (固定/生活/债务/想买), compute disposable cash with formulas, zero-based budget, debt avalanche vs snowball, affordability checks (monthly cost + opportunity cost). Explicit red lines: no investment/tax advice, no bank credentials, no fabricated numbers.
