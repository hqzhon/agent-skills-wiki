## When to use
When you want an agent to turn a song into a paper-collage lyric MV (cut-paper puppets, watercolor backgrounds, Song-type paper lyric cards timed per character)—rendered entirely in code, with no AI image/video model.

## What it does
Codex / Claude Code skill that encodes the design philosophy and pipeline of a hand-tuned paper-collage lyric MV: agent writes canvas/code scenes, syncs lyrics, and outputs ~1080p/24fps. Author publishes side-by-side runs with vs without the skill on the same model/audio.
