## When to use
When someone hands you a **TikTok / Reel / Short file** and asks why it works, what the hook is, how scenes are structured, or how to borrow that structure—scene-by-scene beats (hook/setup/value/proof/payoff/cta) and hold scores.

## What it does
`skills/shortform-analyzer/SKILL.md` plus `npx shortform-analyzer analyze <file>` (needs ffmpeg + `OPENAI_API_KEY`). Outputs summary, `analysis.json`, `report.html`, and `sheet.jpg`. Also usable as a library or MCP server.
