## When to use
When you need any agent to **resume work another session stopped**—set up handoff files, continue a queue, save progress before a new chat, archive a finished round, or report what’s next in the plan.

## What it does
Project-local handoff kit (`setup | continue | save | finish | status | update`) with templates for purpose, current state, improvement plan, and AGENTS.md. Designed so Claude Code / other skill hosts can take over a queue without re-explaining context.
